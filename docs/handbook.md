Welcome to the Bite Bistro Family

Welcome to Bite Bistro! We are thrilled to have you join our team. Our mission is simple: to serve delicious, high-quality food paired with genuine, warm hospitality. At Bite Bistro, we believe that every meal is an opportunity to create a memorable experience for our guests, and as a member of our staff, you are the heart and soul of that experience. We are committed to fostering a supportive, energetic, and respectful workplace where everyone can thrive.

Attendance and Punctuality

Reliability is the cornerstone of our daily operations. Your scheduled shifts are crucial to maintaining the smooth flow of the restaurant, and our guests depend on you being here. If you are running late or need to request time off, you must notify your shift manager as far in advance as possible, and no later than 24 hours before your shift begins, except in cases of documented emergencies. Chronic lateness or unexcused absences will be addressed through our progressive disciplinary policy.

Dress Code and Personal Hygiene

Professional appearance and strict personal hygiene are mandatory for all front-of-house and kitchen staff. You are required to wear your clean, pressed Bite Bistro uniform at all times while on the clock. Long hair must be tied back, and kitchen staff must always wear appropriate headwear and non-slip footwear. Remember to wash your hands frequently, keep fingernails clean and trimmed, and refrain from wearing heavy fragrances or jewelry that could interfere with food safety.

Customer Service Standards

Exceptional customer service is what sets Bite Bistro apart from the competition. Always greet guests with a warm smile within 30 seconds of their arrival and make eye contact when speaking to them. When taking orders, listen actively, answer questions about the menu confidently, and repeat the order back to ensure accuracy. If a guest has a complaint, listen patiently, apologize sincerely for the inconvenience, and resolve the issue promptly or escalate it to a manager on duty.

Food Safety and Sanitation

Food safety is absolutely non-negotiable. All team members must adhere strictly to local health department regulations and our internal safety protocols. This includes proper temperature control for both raw and cooked foods, preventing cross-contamination by using color-coded cutting boards, and sanitizing prep stations regularly throughout your shift. If you are feeling ill, especially with gastrointestinal symptoms, you must notify your manager immediately and stay home.

Workplace Safety and Emergency Procedures

Maintaining a safe work environment is everyone's responsibility. Be constantly mindful of wet floors and immediately clean up any spills using the designated yellow caution signs. If you notice a faulty piece of kitchen equipment, a frayed wire, or a potential hazard, report it to management immediately. All employees must familiarize themselves with the location of fire extinguishers, first-aid kits, and emergency exits, and know the proper evacuation routes.

Breaks and Employee Meals

We value your well-being and understand the importance of rest during a busy shift. All employees working shifts longer than six hours are entitled to a 30-minute unpaid meal break. During this time, you may enjoy a complimentary employee meal from our designated staff menu in the break room. Please ensure you clock out for your breaks and return to your station promptly to relieve your colleagues.

Teamwork and Communication

Effective communication and teamwork are essential to surviving a busy lunch rush or a packed Friday dinner service. Whether you are a server, chef, dishwasher, or busser, you must work together seamlessly. Use clear, professional language when communicating with the kitchen and front-of-house staff. If you need help during a rush, ask for it early rather than waiting until you are overwhelmed. We win as a team and lose as a team.

Point of Sale (POS) and Cash Handling

Proper handling of our Point of Sale (POS) system and cash is critical to the financial health of the bistro. You are responsible for your individual login credentials; never share your password or swipe another employee's card. When processing payments, double-check the order details, confirm the payment method, and always count back change to the guest. Report any technical issues with the POS system or discrepancies in your cash drawer to the floor manager immediately.

Disciplinary Policy and Conclusion

We expect all employees to maintain high standards of conduct, professionalism, and performance. Failure to adhere to the policies outlined in this handbook may result in disciplinary action, which can include verbal warnings, written warnings, suspension, or termination depending on the severity of the infraction. We are excited to have you as part of the Bite Bistro family and look forward to a successful, delicious journey together!
